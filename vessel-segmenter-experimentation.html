Smoothing

Gaussian blur applied before thresholding, in pixels. Raise this to suppress noise/grain on grainy images; too high erodes fine, thin vessels.
Raise this to fuse nearby broken pieces into one continuous selection so a single vessel doesn't get cut off partway. Too high merges separate vessels together.

Threshold

Starts from automatic (Otsu) thresholding. Slide right to select more of a vessel (helps if only part of it is being picked up), left to select less.

Splitting

Automatically separates vessels that are touching or overlapping into distinct regions, by finding the "pinch point" between them (watershed on the shape's distance map) — similar to QuPath's split-by-shape.
Higher = splits more eagerly, even at weak/narrow pinch points (can over-split irregular or elongated vessels). Lower = only separates clearly distinct, roughly round vessels.

Filtering

Detected regions smaller than this (in pixels²) are discarded as noise/debris, not exported as vessels.

Manual editing

Pick a mode above, then click directly on the image.
Delete: click a region to remove it. Merge: click one region, then a second, to combine them into one. Split: click-drag a line across a vessel to cut it into two. Adjusting the sliders above re-runs auto-detection and clears manual edits.
